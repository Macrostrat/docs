For a decade, Macrostrat has integrated the world's geologic maps and stratigraphic
columns into one model of the crust. Today we are opening the beta of **Macrostrat
v2**: a rebuilt site, a rebuilt maps system, and the beginnings of a platform that
geologists can contribute to directly.
