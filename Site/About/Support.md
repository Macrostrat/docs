Macrostrat's free, open, and innovative platform is made possible by a sequence of grants, sustained institutional support, in-kind contributions from partners, and gifts from people who use it.

## Donate

Grants start things and keep us innovating, but a free, open resource has ongoing costs
that fall between grant cycles: certificates and domains, cloud hosting and backups,
security and monitoring, testing devices, and above all the people who curate a very
large dataset and build the software. Individual gifts let us keep the lights on
between awards and prioritize the improvements our users ask for.
A one-time or recurring gift to the [**Paleontology Program Fund in Geology**](https://secure.supportuw.org/give/?id=E0A03FA3-B2A0-431C-83EE-A121A04EEB5D) at the
University of Wisconsin Foundation supports Macrostrat and Rockd directly. For larger gifts, sponsorships or in-kind partnerships, write to us.
