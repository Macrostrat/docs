Macrostrat is free to use and open in every sense: open data, open source, open to
contribution. That is possible because of a sequence of grants, sustained institutional
support, in-kind contributions from partners, and gifts from people who use it.

## How Macrostrat has been funded

**Founding (2012–2016).** Macrostrat began as the data infrastructure of a U.S. National
Science Foundation CAREER award to Shanan Peters (EAR-1150082). EarthCube funding
(ICER-1440312) turned a research database into a public platform: the API, the first
web interface, and the geologic map integration that became Rockd.

**Sustaining (2019–).** Continued NSF support for data acquisition and infrastructure
(EAR-1948843, ICER-1928323) kept the maps and columns growing and the services running.

**Macrostrat v2 (2023–).** Three awards fund the current push toward a
community-contribution platform. An NSF *Elements* award (2311091, with Stanford
2311092) links geochemical proxy records in the Sedimentary Geochemistry and
Paleoenvironments Project to Macrostrat's stratigraphic framework. An NSF *Open-Source
Ecosystem* award (RISE-2324579) builds the shared ecosystem between Macrostrat and
StraboSpot. DARPA's CriticalMAAS program drove map ingestion at a scale that reshaped
the maps system.

**Throughout.** The UW–Madison Department of Geoscience has supported the project
continuously since 2013, including the people who build it. Rockd development is
supported by the AAPG Foundation since 2025.

**In kind.** The UW–Madison Center for High Throughput Computing hosts our computing
infrastructure. Mapbox has supported Rockd's intensive use of its location services
since 2016. xDD provides links from geologic units to the published literature.

## Why give

Grants start things and keep us innovating, but a free, open resource has ongoing costs
that fall between grant cycles: certificates and domains, cloud hosting and backups,
security and monitoring, testing devices, and above all the people who curate a very
large dataset and build the software. Individual gifts let us keep the lights on
between awards and prioritize the improvements our users ask for.

## How to give

A one-time or recurring gift to the **Paleontology Program Fund in Geology** at the
University of Wisconsin Foundation supports Macrostrat and Rockd directly.

[Give through the UW Foundation](https://secure.supportuw.org/give/?id=E0A03FA3-B2A0-431C-83EE-A121A04EEB5D)

For larger gifts, sponsorships or in-kind partnerships, write to us.
