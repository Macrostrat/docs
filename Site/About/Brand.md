Use these assets when you acknowledge Macrostrat in a talk, poster, paper or app.
Please do not alter the marks or recolor them.

## Logos

for small sizes and a dark-background variant. SVG is preferred; PNG is provided for
tools that cannot use SVG.

## Colors

The palette is sampled from the logo: purple primary, with mint, pink and sky accents.

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Purple (primary) | `#492f7a`, `#7e66a9` |
| Pink | `#f073b9`, `#fcd9fa` |
| Mint / teal | `#68ded4`, `#a7e8cd` |
| Sky | `#5b8bd9`, `#b8c9e7` |

These are the brand primitives of Macrostrat's design system (Paleozoic, `--pz-`
variables) and are what the website itself uses.

## Naming

"Macrostrat", one word, capital M. The mobile app is "Rockd". When you mean the
organization, "the Macrostrat lab at the University of Wisconsin–Madison".

## Wordmark and older marks

The v1 wordmark and the pre-2024 logos are retired; please use the marks above.
