## Add your app

Built something on Macrostrat? Add an entry to `Site/data/apps.yml` in the
[documentation repository](https://github.com/Macrostrat/docs) and open a pull request:
a name, a sentence, a link, who made it, and optionally a logo. That is the whole
process.
