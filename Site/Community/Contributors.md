## Acknowledgments

Rockd's first two versions were whiteboarded and coded by John Czaplewski, whose
involvement made the group what it is today. Lab members and participants past and
present, among them Noel Heim, Puneet Kishor, Jon Husson, Andrew Zaffos, Dan
Segessenman, Scott Hartman, Aaron Kufner, Shan Ye, Pat McLaughlin, Dave Lovelace,
Valerie Syverson, Sharon McMullen, Ben Linzmeier, Ben Barnes, Neal Auchter, Annaka
Clement, Erika Ito, Victoria Khoo and Josie Sajbel, contributed to the database and the
research group in many different ways.

Beyond the lab, Phoebe Cohen and Rowan Lockwood described teaching strategies for
using Rockd, and many others have engaged with that approach. Artwork and logos were
provided by Duncan Anderson (Rockd v1 and v2) and Daven Quinn (v3 and the Macrostrat
v2 mark). Shane Loeffler and Ron Schott provided feedback on the Android platform;
Himanshi Shah-Quinn provided UX design and feedback.

Above all, thanks to the geologists who have taken the time to contribute their
observations so that others may engage more fully with the Earth's past.
